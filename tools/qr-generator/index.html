About this tool

Turn text or a URL into a local QR code, with PNG and SVG downloads.

How it works

The bundled Nayuki JavaScript library calculates the QR matrix. Modules use integer pixel sizes and a quiet zone of at least four modules.

FAQ

Why might a long message fail?

QR capacity is finite, and stronger error correction reduces it. Shorten the text or choose a lower correction level.
