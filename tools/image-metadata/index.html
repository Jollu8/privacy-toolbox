About this tool

Inspect and remove EXIF, GPS, and embedded image metadata.

How it works

Pillow first reads metadata for your inspection. Removal creates a new image from oriented pixels, without the original metadata containers.

FAQ

Does this hide what is visible in the photo?

No. Faces, documents, and landmarks in the picture remain visible. GPS coordinates are displayed locally without maps or network lookups.
