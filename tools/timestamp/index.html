About this tool

Convert Unix seconds or milliseconds to dates and back.

How it works

Python converts against the UTC epoch with microsecond precision. The browser also displays the instant in its local timezone.

FAQ

Are negative timestamps supported?

Yes, within years 1–9999. Fractional values are supported up to microsecond precision; the browser local display has millisecond precision.
